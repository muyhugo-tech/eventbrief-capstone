Every accepted record used `agreed` for the reported booking state while the frozen reference uses `stated`. This consistent convention mismatch makes every strict case fail; it is not evidence that every event fact is wrong. Two accepted records appended supported operational commentary to staff notes rather than extracting only the note content. All accepted records matched all five document decisions and the expected quote behavior.

The attendance-conflict record was rejected by local validation: its single quotation contained both conflicting totals, while the contract requires at least two evidence entries. The AI recognized the conflict, returned a null total, and asked which total was correct. The application did not accept the record. Its raw response and token usage remain saved; the failed case receives no accepted-output credit. Do not describe this as choosing an incorrect guest count.

The batch stopped on that failure. After inspecting it, six previously unattempted cases were predeclared in a separate phase using identical extraction settings. The combined baseline includes all original twelve receipts, including the failed conflict case; no favorable retries were selected.

## Limits

These are fictional development fixtures, not a held-out benchmark or evidence of real-world accuracy or time savings. Exact quotation matching does not prove semantic support. Question meaning, evidence support and the full semantic case metric remain unscored pending human review. Keeping staff text out of guest documents is checked against specified literal strings; it does not prove universal privacy protection.
