# Five live demo tests

All people, conversations and arrangements below are fictional. These are demonstration cases, not independent held-out evaluation data.

1. Open the public demo. No code is needed.
2. Choose a test and click **Use this conversation**, then **Prepare five drafts**. Or copy the conversation below and paste it into **New event**.
3. Check all five drafts against the listed expectations.
4. Edit a guest count in **Edit event details** to see the affected drafts update without another AI call.

Exact conversations with saved AI results reopen the real saved result at no extra cost. A changed conversation needs one new AI call. There is no automatic retry or allowance refill. The allergy case intentionally leaves three drafts blocked.

## 1. Birthday dinner — complete details

- 18 guests and 2 children (20 total).
- November 6, 2026, 6–9 PM, Garden Patio, Menu 2.
- All five drafts should be ready for review.

```text
Host — Elena Brooks: Hi! I'd like a birthday dinner at Sergio's Restaurant Venue on November 6, 2026, from 6 PM to 9 PM. We will have 18 guests and 2 children. Could we use the Garden Patio and Menu 2?

Restaurant: Yes, we can arrange the Garden Patio and Menu 2. What would you like for drinks?

Host — Elena Brooks: Nonalcoholic service, please. There are no allergies or dietary restrictions. One long table would be great. No AV or other special requirements.

Restaurant: Confirmed: November 6, 2026, 6–9 PM on the Garden Patio, 18 guests and 2 children, Menu 2, nonalcoholic service and one long table. We have noted no allergies or dietary restrictions.

Host — Elena Brooks: That is all correct. Thank you!
```

## 2. Rehearsal dinner — changed count, no end time

- Use the latest count: 28 guests and 2 children (30 total), not 24 guests.
- Start at 5:30 PM on November 12. Default the missing end time to 9:30 PM.
- All five drafts should use the updated details.

```text

```

## 3. Team dinner — keep juice and soda

- 22 guests and no children, November 18, 6–9 PM.
- Keep juice and soda in the drink details; do not change them to beer or wine.
- All five drafts should be ready; check the quote, staff plan and guest plan for the drinks.

```text
Host — Priya Shah: I'd like to book our team dinner at Sergio's Restaurant Venue on November 18, 2026, from 6 PM to 9 PM. There will be 22 guests and no children. Can we have the Garden Patio and Menu 2?

Restaurant: Yes. What drinks would you like?

Host — Priya Shah: Nonalcoholic service with juice and soda, please. No beer or wine. There are no allergies or dietary restrictions. One long table and no AV are fine.

Restaurant: Confirmed for November 18, 2026, 6–9 PM, Garden Patio, 22 guests and no children, Menu 2, and nonalcoholic service with juice and soda. We will use one long table and have noted no allergies or dietary restrictions.

Host — Priya Shah: Perfect. Please keep the juice and soda in the event plan.
```

## 4. Family dinner — an allergy needs checking

- 26 guests and 2 children, November 22, 5–9 PM.
- Calendar and quote can be reviewed. Kitchen, staff and guest plans should flag the unresolved allergy.
- To finish without another AI call: edit the allergy details, enter the confirmed answer and save a note.

```text
Host — Marcus Reed: We would like a family dinner at Sergio's Restaurant Venue on November 22, 2026, from 5 PM to 9 PM. We have 26 guests and 2 children and would like the Garden Patio, Menu 2 and nonalcoholic service.

Restaurant: We can arrange those details. Are there any allergies or dietary requirements?

Host — Marcus Reed: One guest has a food allergy, but I still need to find out which food. I will check with them and send you the details. Please do not assume there are no allergies.

Restaurant: Understood. The allergy details are still pending. The other details are confirmed: November 22, 2026, 5–9 PM, Garden Patio, 26 guests and 2 children, Menu 2 and nonalcoholic service.

Host — Marcus Reed: Correct. I will get back to you about the allergy.
```

## 5. Anniversary dinner — use the new date

- Use December 5, 2026, not the original December 4.
- 30 guests, no children, 6–10 PM, Menu 2 and beer and wine service.
- All five drafts should use the new date; the calendar must not keep December 4.

```text
Host — Sofia Bennett: Hello! We are planning an anniversary dinner at Sergio's Restaurant Venue for December 4, 2026, from 6 PM to 10 PM. We have 30 guests and no children. We'd like the Garden Patio, Menu 2, beer and wine service, and three tables of ten. There are no allergies or dietary restrictions.

Restaurant: We can arrange those details for December 4.

Host — Sofia Bennett: Could we move it to December 5, 2026, instead? The time, count, menu, drinks and setup are all unchanged.

Restaurant: Yes. The new date is confirmed: December 5, 2026, 6–10 PM, Garden Patio, 30 guests and no children, Menu 2, beer and wine service, three tables of ten, and no allergies or dietary restrictions.

Host — Sofia Bennett: Thank you. December 5 is the date we want. Please disregard December 4.
```
