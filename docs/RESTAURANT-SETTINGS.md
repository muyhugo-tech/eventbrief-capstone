# Restaurant settings

1. Open your private workspace with the access code.
2. Click **Restaurant settings**.
3. Choose **Spaces**, **Menus** or **Drinks**. Change the values.
4. Add a short note under **What changed?** Click **Save restaurant settings**.
5. Open the event again. Check any changed drafts before marking them reviewed.

Saved settings are shared between browsers using the same access code. The public sample stays separate. Saving settings and reopening drafts make no AI call.

For a ready-made fictional setup, expand **Need a fictional rehearsal example?**, click **Fill with rehearsal sample**, check the values, then save. It adds Menu 3 at $64 per guest / $24 per child, a patio limit of 60, and separately billed drinks with a cash bar. These are invented capstone values.

For drinks, **Price per person** adds a priced line to the quote. **Paid separately** excludes drinks from the food estimate and states that clearly on the quote and staff sheet. Optional equivalent wording must match a complete saved instruction; extra promises still need review.

New menus show their dishes on kitchen sheets. The kitchen lead still supplies their prep quantities; the original Menu 2 is the only menu with built-in quantity rules.

Settings do not fill missing event facts. Alex's rehearsal still needs an end time, timezone, guest/child split and the remaining operational notes. His original messages and AI result remain unchanged.
